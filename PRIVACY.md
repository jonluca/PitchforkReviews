# Pitchfork Reviews Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Pitchfork Reviews is a browser extension developed by JonLuca DeCaro. It displays review scores on Pitchfork album-review listing pages.

## Data handling

While the user is visiting Pitchfork, the extension finds links to Pitchfork album reviews, requests those review pages from Pitchfork, extracts the published score, and displays it in the listing. This processing occurs in the browser and is limited to Pitchfork pages.

Pitchfork may receive cookies and network information normally associated with a same-site page request and handles that information under its own privacy policy. No data is sent to the extension developer or to another external service.

## Collection, storage, and sharing

Pitchfork Reviews does not collect, upload, store, sell, or share personal information, browsing history, page content, form data, credentials, or communications. It uses no analytics or advertising services.

## Retention and user control

The extension retains no user data. Removing it ends its access immediately.

## Changes and contact

Material changes to this policy will be reflected in this document and its updated date. Questions may be sent to [chrome@jonlu.ca](mailto:chrome@jonlu.ca).
